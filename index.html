Services

LLMs & agents from 0 to 1

I take an LLM or agent idea from first scoping to a working system your team can run. I build hands-on with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and agent harness engineering.

What I do

  • Scope the use case and define what good looks like.
  • Design the data and retrieval layer.
  • Build the agent harness: tools, orchestration and guardrails.
  • Write evaluation sets, deploy, and hand over.
Examples (typical engagements):
  • A RAG assistant over your internal documents, with cited answers.

Selected work

Route planning for an agricultural cooperative, Denmark

Proof of concept: a web GIS digital twin of depots, customers, trips and tonnages. Planners could re-run scenarios without code. I spent two weeks on site benchmarking against their own routes.

Tools: ArcGIS Network Analyst, ArcPy, Alteryx.

Result: proof-of-concept routes averaged 20% fewer empty kilometres than planner routes; around 10% estimated once constraints the model did not capture were allowed for.

Depot network planning, UK logistics operator

Depot-level demand forecasts, fleet sizing, depot-closure scenarios and route optimisation for 200 trucks and 12 depots.

Tools: LightGBM quantile forecasts, rolling-origin backtesting.

Result: modelling indicated vehicle utilisation could rise from 73% to 80% and tonne-km fall 5%.

Tender models, UK waste and environmental services operator

Route-optimisation and pricing models for public-tender bids. The methods later became local standard practice.

Tools: T-SQL, Excel, Power BI, AMCS Route Planner.

Result: used in winning bids for 7 public-sector contracts worth about GBP 70m a year; bid success rate up 70%.

Fibre rollout prioritisation, Nigeria, South Africa and Ghana

Scored 1 km² grid cells on population density and income, then built ranking dashboards so rollout could be phased by expected return.

Tools: Alteryx, ArcGIS, Tableau.

Renewable siting, Mozambique

Modelled wind and solar supply potential and matched it to demand to show where new developments should go.

Tools: Alteryx, Python, GIS.

Tourism performance BI platform, GCC government authority

An end-to-end platform with validated, harmonised and documented data pipelines.

Tools: Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Python, Alteryx.
